# jMidi 1.0.0

**A new home for your MIDI workflow.**

Version 1.0 brings together practical MIDI editing tools and a dedicated live MIDI In / Out monitor.

## What's included

- **MIDI device overview** — see available input and output ports and the currently selected devices.
- **Live activity** — follow incoming and outgoing MIDI messages as your setup is being used.
- **Message details** — view activity counts, recent-message timestamps and separate IN/OUT event logs.
- **Connection diagnostics** — inspect queue depth and dropped-message counters when troubleshooting.
- **MIDI editing tools** — work with notes, controllers, timing, groove, humanization and MIDI file cleanup.

## Get started

Connect your MIDI hardware or virtual MIDI ports, launch jMidi, and choose the input and output devices that match your setup. Device availability depends on the drivers and MIDI services installed on your computer.
